Rhinoplasty (Nose Reshaping)

Rhinoplasty is a surgical procedure that reshapes the nose to improve its appearance, proportion, and in many cases, breathing function. Whether addressing a bump on the bridge, refining the nasal tip, or correcting asymmetry, rhinoplasty is one of the most transformative facial procedures available.

What to Expect

The procedure typically takes 1.5-3 hours and is performed under general anesthesia. Depending on the complexity, a closed approach (incisions inside the nostrils) or an open approach (with a small incision across the columella) may be used. The surgeon carefully sculpts the bone and cartilage framework to achieve the desired shape while maintaining structural integrity.

Recovery Timeline

  • Week 1: A nasal splint is worn to protect the nose. Bruising around the eyes and swelling are normal. Most patients manage discomfort with prescribed medication.
  • Week 2: The splint is removed. Most bruising fades. Patients can typically return to work or school.
  • Month 1-2: The majority of visible swelling resolves. The nose begins to take its new shape.
  • Month 6-12: Subtle refinement continues as residual swelling fully resolves, especially at the nasal tip. Final results are typically visible by one year.

Ideal Candidates

Rhinoplasty is well-suited for patients who are bothered by the size, shape, or proportion of their nose. Common concerns include a prominent dorsal hump, a wide or bulbous tip, nasal asymmetry, or breathing difficulties caused by a deviated septum. Candidates should be in good overall health and have realistic expectations about outcomes.

Combining Procedures

Rhinoplasty is frequently combined with chin augmentation to optimize facial balance and profile harmony. It can also be performed alongside septoplasty for functional improvement or as part of a comprehensive facial rejuvenation plan. During your consultation, we will discuss how to achieve the most harmonious result.
