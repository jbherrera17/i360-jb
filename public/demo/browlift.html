Brow Lift (Forehead Lift)

A brow lift elevates drooping brows and smooths deep forehead creases and frown lines, restoring a more youthful, rested, and approachable expression.

What to Expect

The procedure takes 1-2 hours using endoscopic (minimal incision) or classic coronal technique. Most patients receive general anesthesia or sedation.

Recovery Timeline

  • Days 1-3: Swelling and mild discomfort. Keep head elevated.
  • Week 1: Sutures removed. Most bruising begins to fade.
  • Week 2-3: Return to work. Temporary numbness at incision site is normal.
  • Month 1-3: Final results emerge as swelling resolves.

Ideal Candidates

Candidates typically experience low, heavy brows creating a tired appearance, deep horizontal forehead creases, or frown lines between the eyebrows.

Combining Procedures

A brow lift is frequently combined with blepharoplasty (eyelid surgery) for upper facial rejuvenation, or with a facelift for a full-face approach.
