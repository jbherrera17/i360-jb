Blepharoplasty (Eyelid Surgery)

Blepharoplasty rejuvenates the area around the eyes by removing excess skin, muscle, and sometimes fat from the upper and lower eyelids. The procedure addresses drooping upper lids, puffy bags beneath the eyes, and excess skin that can impair peripheral vision, restoring a more alert, youthful, and rested appearance.

What to Expect

Upper blepharoplasty takes approximately 1-2 hours and can often be performed under local anesthesia with sedation. Incisions are placed within the natural crease of the upper eyelid, making them virtually invisible once healed. Lower blepharoplasty may use an incision just below the lash line or a transconjunctival approach (inside the lower lid) to address fat pads without visible scarring.

Recovery Timeline

  • Days 1-3: Cold compresses and head elevation help minimize swelling. Eyes may feel tight and mildly sore.
  • Week 1: Sutures are removed. Bruising and swelling begin to subside. Lubricating eye drops are used as needed.
  • Week 2: Most patients return to work and daily activities. Makeup can be applied to conceal residual bruising.
  • Month 1-3: Incision lines continue to fade and blend with the natural eyelid crease. Final results are typically visible within three months.

Ideal Candidates

Blepharoplasty is ideal for patients who notice heavy, sagging upper eyelids that create a tired or aged appearance, under-eye bags that persist regardless of sleep, or excess skin on the upper lids that interferes with vision. Both women and men benefit from this procedure, and it is one of the most popular cosmetic surgeries for patients in their 40s and beyond.

Combining Procedures

Eyelid surgery pairs beautifully with a brow lift to address forehead heaviness that contributes to upper lid sagging. It is also commonly performed alongside a facelift or laser skin resurfacing for a complete facial rejuvenation. Your surgeon will recommend the most effective combination based on your anatomy and goals.
