Neck Lift (Lower Rhytidectomy)

A neck lift, also known as a lower rhytidectomy or platysmaplasty, targets sagging skin, excess fat, and muscle banding in the neck and jawline area. The procedure restores a smooth, defined contour from the chin to the collarbone, eliminating the appearance of a "turkey neck" or double chin that diet and exercise cannot resolve.

What to Expect

The procedure typically takes 2-4 hours under general anesthesia or deep sedation. Incisions are made behind the ears and sometimes under the chin, allowing the surgeon to tighten the platysma muscle, remove or redistribute excess fat via liposuction, and re-drape the skin for a naturally youthful neckline.

Recovery Timeline

  • Week 1: A compression garment is worn to minimize swelling and support healing. Moderate swelling and bruising are expected. Most patients manage discomfort with prescribed medication.
  • Week 2: Sutures are removed. Swelling begins to decrease noticeably. Light daily activities can resume.
  • Week 3-4: Most patients feel comfortable returning to work and social activities. The compression garment may be transitioned to nighttime use only.
  • Month 2-3: Residual swelling resolves and the final neck contour becomes apparent. Incision lines continue to fade over the following months.

Ideal Candidates

Neck lift surgery is ideal for patients who are bothered by loose, sagging neck skin, visible platysmal bands (vertical cords), excess submental fat creating a double chin, or loss of definition along the jawline. Candidates are generally in good health, non-smokers, and have realistic expectations about the outcome.

Combining Procedures

A neck lift is most commonly combined with a facelift to achieve seamless rejuvenation from the forehead to the collarbone. It can also be paired with chin augmentation to improve profile balance, or with submental liposuction for patients whose primary concern is excess fat rather than skin laxity. Your surgeon will design a tailored plan during your consultation.
