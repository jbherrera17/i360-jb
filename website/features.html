AI that respects your values

How do you know AI won't give advice that contradicts your values? What if it recommends something that would hurt a client relationship or violate your ethics?

Insight 360 lets you define "bright lines" - principles the AI should never cross. Your values become guardrails that shape every recommendation. Human oversight, not replacement.

  • Track alignment with your principles over time
