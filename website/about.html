We believe AI should amplify your humanity, not replace it

Synergi AI was founded on a simple observation: small and mid-sized businesses deserve AI tools built for their reality, not watered-down enterprise software.

Our Story

Built by SMBs, for SMBs

We started Synergi AI because we experienced the same frustrations you do. As small business operators ourselves, we tried every AI tool on the market. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot - each promised transformation but delivered confusion.

The question we kept asking ourselves: "Are we in the business of running our business, or in the business of becoming AI experts?"

So we built Insight 360 - the AI platform we wished existed. One that handles the complexity so you don't have to. One that actually knows your business. One that respects your values and protects your data.

Our Vision

A future where businesses operate in flow - where every decision is informed, every system is aligned, and every person is empowered through ethical, human-first AI.

Our Mission

To empower small and mid-sized businesses with AI-driven systems that harmonize human insight and intelligent automation - creating clarity, efficiency, and sustainable growth.

What Guides Us

Our Core Values

These aren't just words on a wall. They're the principles we use to make every decision.

Human-Centered Intelligence

AI exists to serve people, not the other way around. Every feature we build starts with the question: "Does this make someone's life better?"

Ethical Innovation

Just because we can build something doesn't mean we should. We consider the impact of every decision on users, their clients, and society.

Clarity Over Complexity

Simple is hard. We do the hard work so you don't have to navigate complexity. If it takes an expert to understand, we haven't finished building it.

Synergistic Growth

Your success is our success. We're not a vendor selling you software - we're a partner invested in your outcomes.

Continuous Evolution

Kaizen - the art of continuous improvement. We're always learning, always iterating, always getting better. And we help you do the same.

Compassion & Persistence

We understand the struggles of running a small business. We bring empathy to every interaction and persist through challenges alongside you.
