Common Questions

Questions SMBs ask us

Real questions from business owners like you. If yours isn't here, just ask.

I'm not technical. Can I still use i360?

Absolutely. We built i360 specifically for business owners who want results, not a second job learning AI. You don't need to know anything about prompts, models, or APIs. You just describe what you need in plain English, and i360 handles the rest. If you can send an email, you can use i360.

How is this different from just using ChatGPT?

ChatGPT is a general-purpose tool that starts fresh every conversation. i360 is different: it learns your business context, maintains your brand voice, and picks the right AI model for each task automatically. Think of ChatGPT as a smart stranger, and i360 as a knowledgeable team member who understands your business.

What if AI gives me bad advice?

This is exactly why we built the Integrity layer. Every AI recommendation is checked against your business values and goals. If something doesn't align, you'll see a warning. And you always have final say - i360 assists and recommends, but you make the decisions. Human oversight is non-negotiable for us.

Is my data safe? Will AI learn from my information?

Your data belongs to you, period. We use enterprise-grade encryption, and your information is never used to train AI models - not ours, not anyone else's. We're SOC 2 compliant because we take this seriously. When you delete something, it's actually deleted.

How long does it take to see value?

Most of our customers start seeing value within the first week. The initial setup is quick - just answer some questions about your business. From there, i360 gets smarter with every interaction. By week two, it feels like it's known your business for years.

What if I want to cancel?

Cancel anytime with one click. No phone calls, no retention specialists, no hoops. We believe the best way to keep customers is to deliver value, not to make leaving difficult. You can export all your data when you go.

Still have questions?

We'd genuinely love to hear from you. Whether you're ready to get started or just exploring, we're here to help.
