FAQ

Common questions

What if I'm not sure which plan is right for me?

That's exactly what our intro conversation is for. We'll talk through your specific situation and recommend the best fit - even if that means waiting or trying something else first. No pressure sales.

Can I change plans later?

Absolutely. Upgrade anytime and get immediate access to new features. Downgrade at the end of your billing cycle. Your data stays with you either way.

Is there a free trial?

We prefer to start with a conversation rather than a timer. We'll show you the platform, answer your questions, and make sure it's the right fit before you commit anything.

What about data security?

Your data is completely isolated from other customers. We never use your information to train AI models. Enterprise-grade security is included in all plans, not just the expensive ones.

Do you offer discounts for non-profits?

Yes - 25% off for registered non-profits. We also offer discounts for multi-year commitments (up to 15% for 3-year deals). Just mention it when we talk and we'll set it up.

What if it doesn't work for me?

We're not for everyone, and that's okay. If after our conversation and initial setup you don't think it's working, we'll help you export your data and cancel without hassle. No contracts, no exit fees.
