## Executive Summary

Insight 360 is evolving from a multi-LLM orchestration platform into a **values-driven intelligence governance system**. Version 3.5 imports **comprehensive content creation assets from the Content Creation System** into the Thought Leadership module, enabling context-aware content generation with Voice DNA, ICPs, product knowledge, and 10 production-ready skills.
