## Executive Summary

Insight 360 is evolving from a multi-LLM orchestration platform into a **values-driven intelligence governance system**. Version 3.9 implements **Align 120 Interactive Chat & Final Report Generation**, transforming the strategic assessment module from static agent execution to dynamic conversational assessments with comprehensive AI Readiness Report generation.
