## Use Cases with Insight 360

### 1. Campaign Strategy Development
**Goal:** Develop integrated campaign plans with target personas, channels, and messaging

**Workflow:**
1. Define campaign objectives and success metrics
2. Load ICP profiles and Voice DNA as context
3. Use **Strategic Advisor** for campaign framework
4. Use **Research Assistant** for competitive landscape

**Agents Used:** Strategic Advisor, Research Assistant
**Gap Identified:** Dedicated Campaign Strategist Agent needed

---

### 2. Brand Voice Enforcement
**Goal:** Review all content against brand voice guidelines

**Workflow:**
1. Load Voice DNA and brand guidelines as context
2. Submit content for review with **Writing Coach**
3. Check for voice consistency, terminology, tone

**Agents Used:** Writing Coach (with Voice DNA context)
**Gap Identified:** Brand Voice Guardian agent for systematic enforcement

---

### 3. Competitive Intelligence
**Goal:** Track competitor positioning, messaging, and campaigns

**Workflow:**
1. Configure **Research Assistant** with competitor list
2. Enable web search for current intelligence
3. Generate competitive landscape summaries

**Agents Used:** Research Assistant (with search enabled)
**Coverage:** Fully covered

---

### 4. Content Brief Creation
**Goal:** Generate detailed briefs for content team

**Workflow:**
1. Define content topic and objectives
2. Load relevant ICP and positioning context
3. Use **Writing Coach** to structure the brief

**Agents Used:** Writing Coach
**Gap Identified:** Content Brief Generator for standardized briefs

---

### 5. Marketing Performance Analysis
**Goal:** Synthesize campaign data into executive insights

**Workflow:**
1. Gather campaign metrics and data
2. Use **Research Assistant** for synthesis
3. Frame narrative around business impact

**Agents Used:** Research Assistant
**Gap Identified:** Marketing Analytics Agent for specialized analysis

---

### 6. Budget Scenario Planning
**Goal:** Model different budget allocation scenarios

**Workflow:**
1. Define budget parameters and scenarios
2. Use **First Principles Thinker** for trade-off analysis
3. Model ROI implications

**Agents Used:** First Principles Thinker
**Gap Identified:** Marketing Budget Optimizer for channel-specific modeling

---

### 7. ICP Refinement
**Goal:** Analyze customer data to refine ideal customer profiles

**Workflow:**
1. Gather customer success and churn data
2. Use **Research Assistant** to identify patterns
3. Update ICP documentation

**Agents Used:** Research Assistant
**Gap Identified:** ICP Analyst Agent for data-driven persona development

```
1. Target persona analysis
2. Channel recommendations with rationale
3. Messaging framework
4. Success metrics
```

### Brand Voice Review
```
Review this content against our brand voice:

Check for: tone consistency, terminology accuracy, audience alignment
```

```
Provide a competitive analysis of [COMPETITOR]:

- Recent campaign activity
- Strengths and vulnerabilities
- Recommended differentiation
```

```
Analyze our [CAMPAIGN/CHANNEL] performance:
- Key metrics vs. benchmarks
- What's working / what's not
- Optimization recommendations
```
