# Profile Settings - User Guide

The Profile page allows you to manage your personal settings, customize your experience, and track your onboarding progress in Insight 360.

## Profile Information

### Display Name
Your name as it appears throughout the application. This is shown in:
- The navigation sidebar
- Chat messages you send
- Activity logs and reports

### Avatar
Choose an emoji to represent you visually. Click on any emoji in the picker to select it as your avatar.

### Email
Your email address (read-only). This is your login identifier and cannot be changed.

### Role
Your access level in the system (read-only):
- **Admin** - Full access to all features including user management
- **User** - Standard access to all features
- **Viewer** - Read-only access to dashboards and reports

### Department
Select your primary department. This helps personalize your experience in Execute 120 and other department-focused features.

## Preferences

### Dark Mode
Toggle between dark and light themes. Your preference is saved and applied across all pages.

## Getting Started Progress

If you haven't completed the onboarding process, you'll see a progress tracker showing:

1. **Profile Setup** - Complete your profile information
2. **Feature Tour** - Learn about key features
3. **First Workflow** - Run your first workflow

Click "Restart Onboarding" to go through the setup wizard again.

## Saving Changes

Changes are saved when you click the "Save Changes" button. You'll see a confirmation toast when your profile is updated successfully.

## Tips

- **Keep your name professional** - It's visible to other users in shared spaces
- **Set your department** - This helps tailor recommendations and quick prompts
- **Complete onboarding** - The tour introduces features you might not discover on your own

## Need Help?

Click the help icon in the top-right corner for more guidance, or contact your administrator for account-related issues.
