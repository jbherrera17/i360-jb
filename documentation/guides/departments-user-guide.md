# Department Management User Guide

**For:** Insight 360 Users
**Last Updated:** February 2026

---

## Why Departments Matter

Departments let you organize your team, agents, and resources into logical groups that mirror your real organizational structure. They provide the foundation for role-based access, resource assignment, and targeted AI workflows.

---

## What It Does

| Action | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **View Departments** | See all departments with their icons, colors, and status |
| **Create Department** | Add a new department with name, description, icon, and color |
| **Edit Department** | Update department details including name, tagline, and status |
| **Delete Department** | Remove a department (unassigns related users and resources) |
| **Search & Filter** | Find departments by name or description, filter by active/inactive |

---

## Step by Step Use

### Creating a Department

1. Click the **Add Department** button in the top right
2. Fill in the department name (required)
3. Add a description explaining the department's purpose
4. Optionally set a tagline for quick identification
5. Choose an icon and color for visual distinction
6. Set a sort order (lower numbers appear first)
7. Click **Create Department**

### Editing a Department

1. Find the department card you want to edit
2. Click the **Edit** button on the card
3. Modify any fields as needed
4. You can change the status to Active or Inactive
5. Click **Save Changes**

### Deleting a Department

1. Click the **Delete** button on the department card
2. Confirm the deletion in the dialog
3. Note: Users and resources assigned to this department will be unassigned

### Searching & Filtering

1. Use the search bar to find departments by name, description, or tagline
2. Use the status dropdown to show only Active or Inactive departments

---

## Tips & Best Practices

- Use distinct colors and icons for each department to make them easy to identify
- Keep department names concise but descriptive
- Use the sort order to control how departments appear in lists across the platform
- Set unused departments to Inactive rather than deleting them to preserve history
- Add taglines for quick context when departments appear in dropdowns

---

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Department not appearing | Check the status filter - it may be set to Active only |
| Can't create department | Ensure you have admin permissions and the name field is filled |
| Changes not saving | Check your network connection and try refreshing the page |
| Delete failed | The department may have dependent resources - check for linked agents or users |
