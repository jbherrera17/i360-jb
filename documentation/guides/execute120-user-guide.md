# Execute 120 - User Guide

Execute 120 is your department-focused execution hub, designed to help you and your team accomplish work efficiently with AI-powered workflows and specialized agents.

## Overview

Execute 120 organizes your work by department, making it easy to find the right tools and workflows for your specific role. The page automatically personalizes content based on your user profile, showing you the most relevant resources for your department and role level.

### What You'll See

- **Context Assets**: Key documents and knowledge resources for your department
- **Specialized Agents**: AI assistants trained for department-specific tasks
- **Quick Actions**: One-click actions for common tasks
- **Workflow Wizards**: Multi-step guided processes for complex tasks
- **Daily Briefing**: Your personalized daily intelligence summary
- **Strategic Overview** (Executives only): High-level strategy metrics and initiatives

## Automatic Personalization

Execute 120 uses your user profile to personalize your experience:

### Department Auto-Selection
When you open Execute 120, it automatically selects your assigned department based on your user profile. This means:
- Marketing team members see Marketing content first
- Sales team members see Sales content first
- Executives see Executive-level resources

You can still browse other departments by clicking the department tabs, but your "home" department is remembered.

### Role-Based Filtering
Content is filtered based on your business role level:
- **Individual Contributors (ICs)** see operational workflows and agents
- **Supervisors/Managers** see team management resources
- **Directors/Executives** see strategic planning tools and the Strategy Overview card

Some workflows and agents are restricted to certain role levels. If you don't see something you expect, check with your administrator about role-based access.

## Getting Started

### 1. Your Department is Pre-Selected

When you first open Execute 120, your department is automatically selected based on your profile. Available departments:
- **Sales** - Deal management, proposals, objection handling
- **Marketing** - Campaign planning, content creation, brand strategy
- **Operations** - Process documentation, efficiency analysis
- **Finance** - Budgeting, forecasting, investment analysis
- **HR** - Hiring, onboarding, performance management
- **Executive** - Board prep, strategic planning, communication
- **Development** - Technical documentation, architecture reviews
- **Legal** - Contract review, compliance, policy management

### 2. Explore Your Cards

Each card displays content relevant to your department:

#### Context Assets Card
Shows key documents, guidelines, and knowledge resources for your department. Click any asset to view details or use it in a conversation.

#### Agents Card
Featured agents specifically trained for your domain. Click any agent to start a conversation.

**Featured agents** are marked with a purple left border - these are the most useful agents for that department.

#### Quick Actions Card
One-click actions for common department tasks. Click the play button to execute an action immediately.

#### Workflows Card
Multi-step guided processes for complex tasks. Click "Run" to start a workflow.

### 3. Run a Workflow

Workflows guide you through complex, multi-step tasks. Click "Run" on any workflow to start:

1. The workflow will open in a dedicated page
2. Follow each step, providing input when requested
3. Review AI-generated content and request changes
4. Complete the workflow to get your final output

## Workflow Types

### User Input Steps
Fill out forms with the information the workflow needs. Required fields are marked with a red asterisk.

### Agent Chat Steps
The AI will generate content based on your inputs. You can ask follow-up questions or request changes.

### Review Steps
Review the output from previous steps. Confirm it looks good before proceeding.

### Output Steps
View and export the final compiled output from the workflow.

## Quick Prompts

Quick prompts are ready-to-use templates. Click the copy icon to copy one to your clipboard, or click the play icon to execute it directly with an agent.

## Daily Briefing

The Daily Briefing card shows a summary of your personalized daily intelligence:
- Department-specific news and updates
- Key metrics and alerts
- Priority items for attention

Click "Read Full Briefing" to see the complete briefing with all sections.

If no briefing has been generated today, click "Generate Now" to create one.

## Strategic Overview (Executives Only)

If you have an Executive or Director role, you'll see a Strategic Overview card showing:
- **Active Initiatives**: Current strategic initiatives in progress
- **Completed This Quarter**: Initiatives completed in the current quarter
- **Average Progress**: Overall progress across all initiatives

Click "Strategy Hub" to access the full Strategy 120 dashboard.

## Tips for Success

1. **Complete your profile** - Your department and role must be set for personalization to work
2. **Provide detailed input** - The more context you give, the better the AI output
3. **Use workflows for complex tasks** - They ensure you don't miss important steps
4. **Iterate on outputs** - Don't accept the first draft; ask for refinements
5. **Check your briefing daily** - Start each day with your personalized intelligence

## Keyboard Shortcuts

- `Ctrl/Cmd + K` - Quick search
- `Escape` - Close modal or cancel current action

## Troubleshooting

### "No content for your department"
Your department may not have content configured yet. Contact your administrator to add department-specific agents, workflows, and assets.

### Missing cards or features
Some features are role-restricted. Executive-level cards (like Strategic Overview) only appear for users with Executive or Director roles. Check your profile settings.

### Wrong department selected
If the wrong department is selected automatically, update your department in your Profile settings. The page will remember your last manual selection as a fallback.

## Need Help?

Click the help icon in the top-right corner of any page for context-specific guidance.
