# Align 120 User Guide

**For:** Insight 360 Users
**Last Updated:** Wednesday, February 12, 2026

---

## Why Align 120 Is Important

Align 120 is the foundation of your AI transformation journey. Before you can strategize (Strategy 120) or execute (Execute 120), you need to establish your organizational readiness. Without this foundation, AI initiatives often fail due to:

- Unclear values that lead to misaligned AI outputs
- Missing process documentation that AI can't optimize
- Unprepared teams that resist or misuse AI tools
- Brand inconsistency when AI generates content
- Governance gaps that create compliance risks

Align 120 provides:
- **Clarity**: Document your values, vision, and mission
- **Assessment**: Understand your current AI maturity
- **Readiness**: Prepare your team for AI adoption
- **Alignment**: Ensure AI reflects your brand
- **Governance**: Establish policies and accountability

---

## What It Does

Align 120 guides you through 5 modules with specialized AI agents:

| Module | Focus | Key Outputs |
|--------|-------|-------------|
| **AI Audit & Assessment** | Current state analysis | AI Inventory, Risk Assessment, Maturity Score |
| **Business Fundamentals** | Core identity | Values, Vision/Mission, Process Map, KPIs |
| **Team UpSkilling** | People readiness | Skills Matrix, Training Paths, Change Readiness |
| **Brand Alignment** | External consistency | Brand Voice, Trust Messaging, Positioning |
| **Corporate Alignment** | Governance & structure | Stakeholder Map, RACI, Policies |

---

## The Three-Lane Framework

Align 120 is the first lane of the I360 AI Transformation Framework:

```
ALIGN 120 (You Are Here)
    ↓ Outputs: Company Profile, Values, Maturity Score
STRATEGY 120
    ↓ Outputs: BSC, OKRs, Investment Cases
EXECUTE 120
    ↓ Outputs: Content, Workflows, Monitoring
```

Your Align 120 outputs feed directly into Strategy 120 planning.

## Step by Step Use

### Getting Started

1. Navigate to **Align 120** in the sidebar
2. You'll see the Start Session panel
3. Click **Start New Session**
4. Enter your company name
5. The session is created and you proceed to Module 1

### Understanding the Interface

**Module Stepper**: The horizontal progress bar shows all 5 modules
- Numbers indicate module order
- Green checkmarks show completed modules
- Current module is highlighted in purple
- Click any module to navigate directly

**Session Info Bar**: Shows when you're in an active session
- Company name being assessed
- Session start date
- Progress ring showing overall completion

**Agent Cards**: Each module shows its available AI agents
- Icon color indicates model type (opus/sonnet/haiku)
- Click cards to learn more about each agent
- Agents run automatically during assessment

**Output Checklist**: Track deliverables for each module
- Circle = Not yet generated
- Checkmark = Completed

## Module 1: AI Audit & Assessment

**Purpose**: Understand your current AI landscape

**What the agents analyze:**
- Existing AI tools in use across the organization
- Risks and compliance gaps
- Opportunities for AI implementation
- Overall maturity level (1-5 scale)

**How to use:**
1. Click **Run AI Assessment**
2. Wait for agents to analyze your data
3. Review the generated outputs
4. Click **Next Module** when ready

**Outputs Generated:**
- AI Tool Inventory
- Risk Assessment Report
- Opportunity Ranking
- AI Maturity Score

**Tips:**
- Be thorough when providing context
- Include both official and "shadow" AI usage
- Don't worry about low maturity scores - they guide improvement

---

## Module 2: Business Fundamentals

**Purpose**: Define your organizational identity

**What the agents help with:**
- Extracting core values from existing materials
- Synthesizing vision and mission statements
- Mapping business processes
- Analyzing unit economics
- Aligning KPIs to strategic goals

**How to use:**
1. Click **Run AI Assessment**
2. Review and refine generated values/vision/mission
3. Verify process inventory accuracy
4. Click **Next Module** when satisfied

**Outputs Generated:**
- Core Values (articulated and prioritized)
- Vision & Mission Statements
- Process Inventory
- Unit Economics Analysis
- KPI/OKR Alignment Map

**Tips:**
- Values should be authentic, not aspirational platitudes
- Vision is where you're going; Mission is why you exist
- Process mapping doesn't need to be perfect - start high-level

---

## Module 3: Team UpSkilling

**Purpose**: Prepare your people for AI adoption

**What the agents assess:**
- Current skill levels across roles
- Gaps between current and required AI skills
- Organizational readiness for change
- Optimal ways of working with AI

**How to use:**
1. Click **Run AI Assessment**
2. Review skills matrix across departments
3. Examine recommended training paths
4. Click **Next Module** when ready

**Outputs Generated:**
- Skills Matrix (current vs. required)
- Training Path Recommendations
- Change Readiness Assessment
- AI Ways of Working Guide

**Tips:**
- Include both technical and non-technical roles
- Don't overlook AI literacy for leadership
- Change readiness affects implementation speed

---

## Module 4: Brand Alignment

**Purpose**: Ensure AI outputs reflect your brand

**What the agents extract:**
- Brand voice characteristics
- Trust and safety messaging guidelines
- Customer sentiment baseline
- Competitive positioning

**How to use:**
1. Click **Run AI Assessment**
2. Review brand voice extraction
3. Refine trust messaging if needed
4. Click **Next Module** when satisfied

**Outputs Generated:**
- Brand Voice Guide (tone, style, vocabulary)
- Trust & Safety Messaging Framework
- Customer Sentiment Baseline
- Competitive Positioning Analysis

**Tips:**
- Provide existing brand materials for better extraction
- Trust messaging is critical for AI-generated content
- Review sentiment baseline before AI content deployment

---

## Module 5: Corporate Alignment

**Purpose**: Establish governance and accountability

**What the agents create:**
- Stakeholder influence mapping
- RACI for AI governance
- Draft AI policies
- Portfolio prioritization framework

**How to use:**
1. Click **Run AI Assessment**
2. Review stakeholder map for completeness
3. Verify RACI assignments
4. Review policy drafts
5. Click **Complete Session**

**Outputs Generated:**
- Stakeholder Map (influence/interest grid)
- Governance RACI Matrix
- AI Policy Drafts (ethics, usage, data)
- Portfolio Priority Framework

**Tips:**
- Include all relevant stakeholders, not just executives
- RACI should be actionable, not theoretical
- Policy drafts are starting points - customize to your context

---

## Completing Your Session

After finishing all 5 modules:

1. Click **Complete Session** in Module 5
2. System generates your Company Profile
3. You're redirected to Company Dashboard
4. All outputs are now available for Strategy 120

**What happens next:**
- Company Profile is created/updated
- All assessment outputs are stored
- Data feeds into Company Dashboard
- You can now proceed to Strategy 120 (S2E)

---

## Resuming Previous Sessions

If you need to continue a previous session:

1. Go to the Align 120 page
2. Scroll to **Previous Sessions**
3. Click on the session you want to resume
4. You'll continue from where you left off

Session cards show:
- Company name
- Start date
- Current module
- Completion percentage

## Navigation Tips

**Jumping Between Modules:**
- Click any module number in the stepper
- Completed modules show green checkmarks
- You can revisit completed modules

**Going Back:**
- Click the **Back** button in any panel
- Or click a previous module number

**Progress Tracking:**
- Watch the progress ring in the session info bar
- Each module completion adds 20%
- 100% means all modules complete

## Tips for Best Results

### Before Starting
- Gather existing documentation (brand guides, org charts, policies)
- Identify key stakeholders who should be involved
- Set aside dedicated time for each module

### During Assessment
- Be honest about current state
- Don't skip difficult questions
- Review AI-generated outputs critically

### After Completion
- Share outputs with relevant stakeholders
- Use Company Dashboard to track ongoing metrics
- Revisit Align 120 annually or after major changes

## Troubleshooting

**Session won't start:**
- Ensure you enter a company name
- Check internet connection
- Refresh the page and try again

**Agents not appearing in module:**
- Verify Align 120 agents are seeded in database
- Check the Agents page for suite="align120" agents
- Contact administrator if agents are missing

**Assessment seems stuck:**
- Wait for the loading overlay to complete
- Large assessments may take 30-60 seconds
- Check browser console for errors

**Can't proceed to next module:**
- Run the assessment first (click Run AI Assessment)
- Wait for all outputs to generate
- Check that output checkmarks appear

**Previous session not showing:**
- Sessions are user-specific
- Ensure you're logged in as the same user
- Check database for align120_sessions records

**Company Profile not created:**
- All 5 modules must be completed
- Click "Complete Session" in Module 5
- Check for errors in the completion dialog
