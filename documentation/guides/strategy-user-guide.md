# Strategy-to-Execution (S2E) User Guide

**For:** Insight 360 Users
**Last Updated:** Wednesday, February 12, 2026

---

## Why S2E Is Important

Strategy-to-Execution (S2E) bridges the gap between high-level vision and daily work. Without this connection, strategies become documents that gather dust while teams execute disconnected tasks.

S2E provides:
- **Clear line of sight**: From vision to individual OKRs
- **Accountability**: Every goal links to strategic purpose
- **Alignment**: Ensure all work serves the bigger picture
- **Measurement**: Track strategy health through linked metrics

---

## What It Does

The S2E module enables:

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Foundation** | Define vision, mission, and core values |
| **Themes** | Establish 2-4 strategic focus areas |
| **BSC Objectives** | Set objectives across four perspectives |
| **OKR Linkage** | Connect OKRs to strategic objectives |
| **Strategy Map** | Visualize the complete cause-effect chain |

---

## Step by Step Use

### Understanding the S2E Process

The S2E workflow follows five sequential steps:

1. **Foundation** → Define your "North Star"
2. **Themes** → Choose your big bets
3. **BSC Objectives** → Balance across perspectives
4. **OKR Linkage** → Connect execution to strategy
5. **Strategy Map** → See the complete picture

### Step 1: Strategic Foundation

1. Navigate to **Strategy** in the sidebar
2. The Foundation panel opens by default
3. Enter your **Vision Statement**:
   - What does success look like in 3-5 years?
   - Make it aspirational and inspiring
4. Enter your **Mission Statement**:
   - Why does your organization exist?
   - What value do you create?
5. Set the **Planning Period** (e.g., "2026-2028")
6. Select your **Vision Horizon** (1-3, 3-5, or 5-10 years)
7. Add **Core Values**:
   - Click "Add Core Value"
   - Enter the value name
   - Add optional description
8. Click **Save & Continue**

### Step 2: Strategic Themes

1. The Themes panel opens after Step 1
2. Click **Add Strategic Theme**
3. Fill in the theme details:
   - **Theme Name**: Clear, memorable identifier
   - **Description**: What this focus area means
   - **Strategic Rationale**: Why it's critical now
   - **Icon**: Visual identifier
   - **Color**: Theme color coding
4. Click **Save**
5. Add 2-4 themes (more dilutes focus)
6. Click **Continue to BSC**

**Good Theme Examples:**
- Customer Excellence
- Operational Efficiency
- Innovation Leadership
- Talent Development

### Step 3: BSC Objectives

The Balanced Scorecard has four perspectives:

| Perspective | Focus | Question |
|-------------|-------|----------|
| **Financial** | Results | How do we look to shareholders? |
| **Customer** | Value | How do customers see us? |
| **Process** | Operations | What must we excel at? |
| **Learning** | Growth | How do we continue to improve? |

**Adding Objectives:**
1. Find the relevant perspective card
2. Click **Add Objective**
3. Enter the objective name (directional, not metric)
4. Add description
5. Optionally link to a theme
6. Click **Save**
7. Add 2-3 objectives per perspective
8. Click **Continue to OKR Linkage**

**Objective Writing Tips:**
- Use directional language ("Increase customer satisfaction")
- Avoid numbers in objectives (numbers belong in OKRs)
- Focus on "what" not "how much"

### Step 4: OKR Linkage

This step connects your existing OKRs to strategic objectives:

1. View the linkage table showing all OKRs
2. For each unlinked OKR:
   - Click the **Link** icon
   - Select the strategic objective it supports
   - Choose the link type:
     - **Supports**: Directly advances the objective
     - **Measures**: Quantifies the objective
     - **Enables**: Creates capability for the objective
     - **Validates**: Tests the objective's achievement
   - Describe how it contributes
   - Click **Link**
3. Continue until key OKRs are linked
4. Click **View Strategy Map**

**Link Status Indicators:**
- **Green**: Linked to an objective
- **Yellow**: Unlinked (needs attention)

### Step 5: Strategy Map

The Strategy Map shows your complete strategic framework:

1. View the cause-effect chain from bottom to top:
   - Learning → Process → Customer → Financial
2. See objectives organized by perspective
3. View OKR counts for each objective
4. Review alignment metrics:
   - **Alignment Rate**: % of OKRs with strategic links
   - **Coverage**: Objectives with linked OKRs
5. Click **Activate Strategy** when complete

## Navigation Between Steps

**Using the Stepper:**
- Click any step number to jump directly
- Green checkmarks indicate completed steps
- Current step is highlighted
- Progress is saved automatically

**Going Back:**
- Click the **Back** button in any panel
- Or click a previous step number
- Changes are preserved

## Tips for Best Results

### Foundation Excellence
- Invest time in vision/mission clarity
- Core values should be actionable, not platitudes
- Review annually for relevance

### Theme Selection
- Less is more (2-4 themes maximum)
- Themes should be mutually reinforcing
- Each theme should have clear ownership

### Objective Quality
- Objectives describe direction, not targets
- Every objective should be verifiable
- Balance internal and external focus

### OKR Alignment
- Not every OKR needs strategic linkage
- Focus on high-impact connections
- Review linkages quarterly

## Troubleshooting

**Can't proceed to next step:**
- Complete all required fields
- Save current step first
- Check for validation errors

**Objectives not appearing in linkage:**
- Verify objectives were saved in Step 3
- Refresh the page
- Check perspective assignments

**OKRs not showing in linkage table:**
- OKRs must exist in Parthenon first
- Check department filter
- Verify OKR status (active vs. archived)

**Strategy Map empty:**
- Complete Steps 1-4 first
- Ensure objectives exist
- Check foundation is active
