# Thought Leadership User Guide

**For:** Insight 360 Users
**Last Updated:** March 16, 2026

---

## Why Thought Leadership Matters

The Thought Leadership module helps you build authority and visibility in your industry through consistent, high-quality content. Instead of struggling to create content from scratch, you get AI-powered assistance that maintains your unique voice while maximizing your reach.

Key benefits:
- **Consistent Output**: Generate a weekly article plus 5 LinkedIn posts in one click
- **AI Visibility**: Optimize content for AI search engines
- **Voice Preservation**: Maintain your authentic perspective
- **Time Efficiency**: Publish a complete content package in under 3 minutes

## Page Layout: Two Modes

The Thought Leadership page has two modes designed around how you actually work:

| Mode | When You Use It | How to Access |
|------|----------------|---------------|
| **This Week** (default) | Every week — generate and publish content | Landing page (automatic) |
| **Settings** | Occasionally — configure positioning, pillars, calendar, integrations | Click the gear icon in the header |

### This Week Mode

This is what you see when you open the page. It answers one question: **"What do I need to do this week?"**

The page shows:
1. **Content Card** — This week's planned article topic, pillar, format, and editorial context (auto-populated from your calendar)
2. **Generate Card** — Integration status, pipeline steps, and the "Generate This Week's Package" button
3. **Results Card** — Your generated article, AI version, header image, and LinkedIn posts with preview/edit/publish controls
4. **Upcoming** — The next 2-3 weeks of planned content
5. **Recent** — Your last 8 weeks with publish status and streak counter

### Settings Mode

Click the gear icon (top right) to access 5 configuration tabs:
- **Position** — Your core thesis and atomic claim
- **Pillars** — 3-5 content themes
- **Calendar** — 52-week editorial calendar with Notion sync
- **Visibility** — AI visibility score and research
- **Publishing & Integrations** — LLM model, Voice DNA, ICP, publish targets

Click **"Back to This Week"** to return to execution mode.

## Getting Started (First-Time Setup)

New users see a 4-step Setup Wizard before accessing the main interface:

### Step 1: Niche Discovery
- Chat with the Strategy Architect agent
- Define your expertise, target audience, and unique angle
- Takes about 5-6 exchanges

### Step 2: Core Positioning
- Craft your core thesis (2-3 sentences)
- Create your atomic claim (one memorable sentence)
- Based on your Step 1 findings

### Step 3: Content Pillars
- Design 3-5 content themes that support your thesis
- Each pillar gets a name, description, and example topics

### Step 4: Visibility Baseline
- Establish your current AI visibility score
- Get initial recommendations for improvement

After completing all 4 steps, you transition to the "This Week" view.

## Weekly Workflow

This is your typical weekly process — designed to take under 3 minutes:

### 1. Review Your Content Card

When you open the page, the Content Card shows this week's planned topic:
- **Title** — from your editorial calendar
- **Pillar** — indicated by a colored left border
- **Format** — Long (2000+ words), Medium (1000-1500), or Short (500-800)
- **Theme** — monthly theme and quarterly pillar context
- **Cornerstone** — reference to the foundation article this extends

If no content is planned for this week, you'll see an empty state with a link to Calendar Settings.

### 2. Check Integration Status

The Generate Card shows pre-flight status dots that load automatically:
- **Green dot + "Ready"** — integration working
- **Yellow dot + warning** — degraded (e.g., expiring credentials)
- **Red dot + error** — unavailable or not configured

Integrations checked: Image API, Notion, Social Media, Substack.

### 3. Generate Your Package

Click **"Generate This Week's Package"** to create everything at once:

| Step | What's Created | Typical Time |
|------|---------------|-------------|
| Article | Full article in your chosen format | ~30s |
| AI Version | Structured version with YAML front matter | ~10s |
| Image | AI-generated header image | ~20s |
| LinkedIn | 5 daily posts (Mon-Fri themes) | ~20s |
| Quality | 7-gate quality checklist | ~5s |

Each step shows real-time status:
- Empty circle — not started
- Spinning circle — in progress (with elapsed time)
- Green check — completed
- Red X — failed (with Retry button)

**If a step fails**, click its Retry button. Completed steps are preserved — you never lose work because of a downstream failure.

### 4. Review Your Results

After generation, the Results Card appears with your complete package:

| Output | Actions |
|--------|---------|
| **Article** | Preview (rendered), Edit (markdown), word count |
| **AI Version** | Preview (structured with YAML) |
| **Header Image** | Preview, Regenerate |
| **LinkedIn Posts** | Preview (5 daily posts with hashtags) |

The Quality Checklist shows pass/fail for:
- Format length, Voice DNA patterns, Cornerstone link, AI optimization, LinkedIn character limits, Hashtag compliance, Actionable insights

### 5. Publish

Select your publish targets (checkboxes):
- **Blog** — public article page with SEO meta tags
- **Notion** — full page in Content Calendar database
- **Social** — via Postiz to LinkedIn, X, Facebook, and more
- **Substack** — newsletter post (beta)

Click **"Publish Package"** — results show per-target success/failure inline.

## Reviewing Past Content

You can review any week's content — not just the current week:

1. **Click a week** in the Upcoming or Recent list
2. The Content Card updates to show that week's topic
3. If content was already generated, the **Results Card auto-populates** with the stored article, image, and LinkedIn posts
4. You can Preview, Edit, or Regenerate from the same interface
5. The Generate button changes to **"Regenerate Package"**

This lets you review your article, header image, and marketing posts all in one place without switching pages.

## LinkedIn Post Framework

Each week generates 5 posts following a daily theme:

| Day | Theme | Purpose |
|-----|-------|---------|
| **Monday** | Insight Launch | Lead with counterintuitive insight |
| **Tuesday** | Problem Spotlight | Highlight the pain point |
| **Wednesday** | Framework Reveal | Share the practical how-to |
| **Thursday** | Story/Example | Make it concrete with narrative |
| **Friday** | Call to Reflect | End with meaning and reflection |

Each post includes a hook line, 150-250 words, engagement question, and 3-5 hashtags.

## Editorial Calendar

Your 52-week editorial calendar follows this hierarchy:

```
Annual Theme
  Quarterly Pillar (13 weeks each)
    Monthly Theme (4 weeks each)
      Week 1: Cornerstone (Long, foundation article)
      Week 2: Extension 1 (Medium, deeper dive)
      Week 3: Extension 2 (Medium, adjacent application)
      Week 4: Practical Guide (Short, actionable checklist)
```

Manage the calendar in **Settings > Calendar** tab. Sync with Notion using the Sync button.

## Tips for Best Results

### Weekly Workflow
- Open the page at the same time each week (Monday morning recommended)
- Review the pre-populated content card before generating
- Preview all outputs before publishing
- Check your streak counter to maintain consistency

### Content Strategy
- Stick to 3-5 pillars for focused authority
- Follow the 4-week pattern: Cornerstone, Extension, Extension, Practical
- Build pillar depth over breadth
- Connect every article back to your atomic claim

### LinkedIn Optimization
- Post at consistent times (use the scheduling feature)
- Engage with comments within 1 hour
- The daily theme framework creates variety while maintaining coherence
- Test different hook styles by editing before publishing

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| **No content card showing** | Check that your editorial calendar has entries for the current year (Settings > Calendar) |
| **Generate button disabled** | Wait for the content card to load; if no entry exists for this week, create one in Calendar Settings |
| **Step fails during generation** | Click the Retry button on the failed step; completed steps are preserved |
| **Image not generating** | Run pre-flight checks; verify your preferred image model is accessible |
| **Notion sync failing** | Verify API key and database ID in Settings > Publishing & Integrations |
| **Substack cookies expired** | Refresh session cookies in Settings > Publishing & Integrations |
| **Results not showing for past week** | Click the week in the Recent list; if content exists, Results Card will auto-populate |
| **Settings changes not reflected** | Click "Back to This Week" to reload the main view with updated configuration |
