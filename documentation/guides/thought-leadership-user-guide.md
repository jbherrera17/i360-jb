**For:** Insight 360 Users
**Last Updated:** January 12, 2026

## Why Thought Leadership Matters

The Thought Leadership module helps you build authority and visibility in your industry through consistent, high-quality content. Instead of struggling to create content from scratch, you get AI-powered assistance that maintains your unique voice while maximizing your reach.

Key benefits:
- **Consistent Output**: Generate a weekly article plus 5 LinkedIn posts
- **AI Visibility**: Optimize content for AI search engines
- **Voice Preservation**: Maintain your authentic perspective
- **Time Efficiency**: Reduce content creation time by 70%

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Run Niche Discovery

Before creating content, establish your thought leadership position:

1. Click **Run Niche Discovery** button
2. Follow the 12-step guided workflow
3. Answer questions about your expertise and audience
4. Review generated thesis and atomic claim
5. Approve or refine your positioning

The Niche Discovery workflow helps you:
- Identify your unique angle
- Define your ideal audience
- Craft your core thesis
- Create your atomic claim

### Step 2: Create Content Pillars

Content pillars are the 3-5 themes you'll consistently write about:

1. Click **Add Pillar** in the pillars section
2. Enter pillar details:
   - **Name**: Short identifier (e.g., "AI Strategy")
   - **Description**: What this pillar covers
   - **Key Topics**: Specific subjects within this pillar
   - **Hashtags**: Social media tags for this content
3. Click **Save**
4. Repeat for each pillar (recommended: 3-5 pillars)

**Pillar Ideas by Industry:**
- Technology: AI/ML, Digital Transformation, Future of Work
- Marketing: Brand Strategy, Content Marketing, Customer Experience
- Leadership: Change Management, Team Building, Innovation

### Step 3: Set Up Your Calendar

The content calendar helps you plan and track content:

1. Navigate using the month arrows
2. Click any day to add a calendar entry
3. Set the entry details:
   - Title
   - Content pillar
   - Scheduled date
   - Status
4. **Sync with Notion** (optional):
   - Click **Sync Notion** button
   - Entries appear in your Notion content calendar
   - Changes sync bidirectionally

## Weekly Content Pipeline

The Weekly Pipeline is your main workflow for content creation:

### Starting the Pipeline

1. Click **Start Weekly Pipeline** button
2. The pipeline guides you through 6 stages:

| Stage | What Happens |
|-------|--------------|
| **Select Topic** | Choose from your pillars or enter custom topic |
| **Research** | AI researches current data and perspectives |
| **Write Article** | Generate long-form article |
| **Review** | Edit and approve the article |
| **LinkedIn Posts** | Generate 5 daily posts from article |
| **Publish** | Schedule and publish content |

### Understanding Pipeline Status

Each stage shows its status:
- **Not started**: Haven't begun this stage
- **In progress**: Currently working on this stage
- **Completed**: Stage finished successfully
- **Pending**: Waiting for previous stage

## Creating Content

### Generating Articles

You can generate articles in three ways:

**Via Pipeline:**
1. Start the Weekly Pipeline
2. Complete Research stage
3. Article is generated automatically

**Via Quick Action:**
1. Click **Write Article** quick action card
2. Chat with the Article Writer agent
3. Provide topic and context
4. Receive completed article

**Via API:**
1. Use the generation endpoints directly
2. Useful for automation and integrations

### Article Formats

Choose your article length:

| Format | Length | Best For |
|--------|--------|----------|
| **Short** | 500-800 words | Quick insights, LinkedIn articles |
| **Medium** | 1000-1500 words | Standard blog posts |
| **Long** | 2000+ words | Comprehensive guides, pillar content |

### Generating LinkedIn Posts

After creating an article, generate your weekly LinkedIn series:

1. Click **Generate LinkedIn** quick action
2. The AI creates 5 posts following this framework:

| Day | Theme | Purpose |
|-----|-------|---------|
| **Monday** | Insight Launch | Lead with counterintuitive insight |
| **Tuesday** | Problem Spotlight | Highlight the pain point |
| **Wednesday** | Framework Reveal | Share the practical how-to |
| **Thursday** | Story/Example | Make it concrete with narrative |
| **Friday** | Call to Reflect | End with meaning and reflection |

Each post includes:
- Hook line (under 10 words)
- 150-250 words of content
- Engagement question
- 3-5 relevant hashtags

## Content Calendar Management

### Adding Entries

1. Click **Add Entry** button
2. Fill in entry details:
   - **Title**: Working title for the content
   - **Pillar**: Content pillar category
   - **Scheduled Date**: Target publish date
   - **Status**: Current state of the content

### Entry Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Idea** | Topic captured, not started |
| **Researching** | Gathering information |
| **Drafting** | Writing in progress |
| **Review** | Ready for editing |
| **Ready** | Approved for publishing |
| **Published** | Live on platform |

### Notion Sync

If you use Notion for content planning:

1. Configure your Notion database ID in settings
2. Click **Sync Notion** to push/pull entries
3. Changes in either system sync automatically
4. Conflicts show both versions for resolution

## Managing Your Position

### Editing Your Thesis

1. Click **Edit Position** button
2. Modify your core thesis
3. Update your atomic claim
4. Save changes

### Your Core Thesis
Your thesis is the central argument you're building. It should:
- Be specific to your expertise
- Challenge conventional thinking
- Provide unique value

**Example:** "AI success isn't about technology—it's about aligning AI capabilities with organizational values."

### Your Atomic Claim
The atomic claim is your thesis distilled to one sentence:
- Memorable and quotable
- Supports your full thesis
- Creates curiosity

**Example:** "Values-aligned AI outperforms pure optimization by 3x."

## Tips for Best Results

### Content Strategy
- Stick to 3-5 pillars for focused authority
- Alternate between pillars weekly
- Build pillar depth over breadth
- Connect pillars to your atomic claim

### Writing Process
- Use research findings for credibility
- Include specific examples and data
- End with actionable takeaways
- Maintain consistent voice across content

### LinkedIn Optimization
- Post at consistent times
- Engage with comments within 1 hour
- Use the daily theme framework
- Test different hook styles

### Building Visibility
- Publish AI-optimized articles on your website
- Include proper metadata
- Use consistent author attribution
- Cross-reference between articles

## Troubleshooting

**Content not generating:**
- Ensure profile is set up
- Check that pillars are defined
- Verify topic is provided

**LinkedIn posts not parsing:**
- Review raw response in output
- Manually extract posts if needed
- Report issue for AI refinement

**Notion sync failing:**
- Verify API key is configured
- Check database ID is correct
- Ensure proper permissions

**Visibility score not updating:**
- Run new visibility check
- Allow 24-48 hours between checks
- Verify web search is working
