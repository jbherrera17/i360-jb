# My AI Capabilities User Guide

**For:** Insight 360 Users
**Last Updated:** Wednesday, February 12, 2026

---

## Why My Capabilities Is Important

My Capabilities is your personalized dashboard showing all AI tools you can access based on your:

- **Role**: Your business role level determines base access
- **Department**: Department assignments provide additional tools
- **Responsibilities**: Selected duties unlock recommended capabilities
- **Direct Assignments**: Custom access granted by administrators
- **Tag Matches**: Access via matching tags between your titles and AI tools

Think of it as your personal AI toolkit—everything you're authorized to use in one place.

---

## What It Does

The My Capabilities page lets you:

| Action | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **View** | See all AI tools available to you |
| **Filter** | Sort by type (agents, skills, workflows, actions) |
| **Access** | Click to use any capability directly |
| **Understand** | See why you have access (role, department, etc.) |

---

## Step by Step Use

### Viewing Your Capabilities

1. Click **My Capabilities** in the sidebar (top-level navigation item)
2. View the summary cards showing counts by type:
   - Agents you can use
   - Skills available to you
   - Workflows you can execute
   - Actions you can perform
3. Browse the capability cards below

### Filtering Capabilities

1. Use the **filter chips** (pill-shaped buttons) at the top
2. Click a capability type:
   - **All** - Show everything
   - **Agents** - AI assistants
   - **Skills** - Reusable skill definitions
   - **Workflows** - Multi-step automations
   - **Actions** - Parthenon actions
3. The list updates to show only that type

### Understanding Access Sources

Each capability card shows:
- **Name**: The capability's name
- **Type**: Agent, Skill, Workflow, or Action
- **Description**: What it does
- **Access Source**: Why you have access:
  - `Role` - Via your business role level
  - `Department` - Via department assignment
  - `Responsibility` - Via selected duties
  - `Direct` - Direct assignment
  - `Tag Match` - Access via matching tags

### Using a Capability

1. Find the capability you want
2. Click the capability card
3. You'll be taken to the appropriate interface:
   - Agents → Agent chat
   - Skills → Skill details
   - Workflows → Workflow runner
   - Actions → Action execution

## Tips for Best Results

- **Check regularly**: New capabilities may be added as your role evolves
- **Explore by type**: Use filters to discover tools for specific tasks
- **Note featured items**: Items marked "Featured" are recommended for your department
- **Request access**: If you need a capability you don't have, contact your administrator

---

## What's Happening Behind the Scenes

When you view this page, Insight 360:

1. Queries your user profile for role and department
2. Retrieves all junction table mappings (agent_roles, skill_roles, etc.)
3. Checks responsibility-AI recommendations
4. Matches your tags against AI tool tags
5. Applies any direct assignments (grants/revokes)
6. Aggregates into a unified capability list

## Common Questions

**Q: Why don't I see a capability I used to have?**
A: Your role, department, or responsibilities may have changed. Contact your administrator.

**Q: Can I request access to new capabilities?**
A: Yes, speak with your department head or administrator about individual assignments.

**Q: What's the difference between "Featured" and regular capabilities?**
A: Featured items are specifically recommended for your department's workflows.

**Q: How often is this list updated?**
A: The list updates in real-time as you load the page.
