# Strategy 120 User Guide

## Welcome to Strategy 120

Strategy 120 is your AI-powered strategic planning companion within Insight 360. It combines the Balanced Scorecard (BSC) framework with OKRs (Objectives & Key Results) to create a powerful strategic alignment system.

## Getting Started

### Accessing Strategy 120

1. **From the sidebar**: Click "Strategy 120" in the navigation menu
2. **From Strategy (S2E) page**: Expand the "Strategy 120 AI Agents" panel
3. **Direct URL**: Navigate to `/strategy120`

### Understanding the Interface

The Strategy 120 page has three main areas:

1. **Agent Categories Sidebar** (left) - Filter agents by their strategic function
2. **Main Panel** (center) - View agents, initiatives, decisions, and intelligence
3. **Tab Navigation** - Switch between Agents, Initiatives, Decisions, and Intelligence

## Using AI Agents

### Starting a Conversation

1. **Find an agent**: Browse categories or use "All Agents" view
2. **Click the agent card**: Opens the chat interface
3. **Ask your question**: Type naturally and press Enter

### Example Conversations

**With Strategy Map Designer:**
> "Help me design a strategy map that connects our customer satisfaction objectives to financial outcomes."

**With Business Case Builder:**
> "I need to build a business case for implementing AI-powered customer service. Our current cost is $2M/year with 50 FTEs."

**With Decision Framer:**
> "We're deciding between building vs. buying our analytics platform. Help me structure this decision."

**With Drift Detector:**
> "Review our Q3 performance against our original strategy and identify any areas of drift."

### Context Awareness

Agents automatically receive context about:
- Your current strategic foundation (vision, mission)
- Strategic themes and BSC perspectives
- Active OKRs and their progress
- Recent initiatives and decisions

This means you can ask follow-up questions without repeating background information.

## Managing Initiatives

### What is an Initiative?

An initiative is a strategic investment project that advances your BSC objectives. Examples:
- "Implement AI-powered customer support chatbot"
- "Launch customer loyalty program"
- "Automate financial reporting"

### Creating an Initiative

1. Go to the **Initiatives** tab
2. Click **New Initiative**
3. Fill in:
   - **Name**: Clear, actionable title
   - **Description**: What will be delivered
   - **BSC Perspective**: Financial, Customer, Process, or Learning
   - **Priority**: 0-100 scale
   - **Status**: Ideation, Planning, Active, etc.

### Initiative Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Ideation | Initial concept, not yet approved |
| Planning | Being scoped and resourced |
| Approved | Ready to execute |
| Active | Currently in progress |
| Completed | Successfully delivered |
| Cancelled | Stopped, won't proceed |
| On Hold | Temporarily paused |

### Linking Initiatives to OKRs

Use the **Business Case Builder** agent to help:
1. Identify which OKRs the initiative supports
2. Estimate impact on key results
3. Document the contribution logic

## Logging Decisions

### Why Log Decisions?

Decision logging creates an audit trail of strategic choices, enabling:
- Learning from past decisions
- Understanding decision rationale
- Reviewing outcomes vs. expectations
- Supporting governance requirements

### Creating a Decision Log Entry

1. Go to the **Decisions** tab
2. Click **Log Decision**
3. Document:
   - **Title**: What was decided
   - **Category**: Strategic, Investment, Resource, etc.
   - **Options Considered**: What alternatives were evaluated
   - **Selected Option**: What was chosen
   - **Rationale**: Why this option was selected
   - **Review Date**: When to revisit

### Using Decision Support Agents

The **Decision Framer** agent can help structure complex decisions:

> "Help me structure our technology platform decision. We're considering three vendors with different pricing models and capabilities."

The **Assumption Tester** agent will challenge your thinking:

> "What assumptions are we making about customer adoption rates? How might those be wrong?"

Each intelligence brief has a relevance score (0-100) indicating its importance to your strategy:
- **80-100**: Critical - requires immediate attention
- **60-79**: Important - should influence planning
- **40-59**: Relevant - worth monitoring
- **0-39**: Awareness only - file for reference

## The OKR-BSC Cascade

### Understanding the Cascade

Strategy 120 tracks alignment from high-level strategy down to individual actions:

```
Vision & Mission
    ↓
Balanced Scorecard Themes
    ↓
Company OKRs (organization-wide goals)
    ↓
Department OKRs (team-level goals)
    ↓
Individual OKRs (personal goals)
```

### Alignment Scores

Each link in the cascade has an alignment score:
- **90-100**: Perfectly aligned
- **70-89**: Well aligned
- **50-69**: Moderate alignment, may need attention
- **Below 50**: Misaligned, requires review

### Checking Alignment

Use the **BSC-OKR Cascade Validator** agent:
> "Review the alignment between our Customer Perspective objectives and our Sales team OKRs."

## Best Practices

### 1. Start with Context

Before asking an agent a question, ensure your strategic foundation is up to date:
- Vision and mission are defined
- Strategic themes are set
- BSC perspectives have objectives
- OKRs are current

### 2. Use the Right Agent

Match your need to the agent's specialty:
- Planning questions → Planning agents
- Financial analysis → Financial Perspective agents
- Decision help → Decision Support agents

### 3. Iterate with Agents

Don't expect perfect answers on the first try:
1. Ask an initial question
2. Review the response
3. Refine or follow up
4. Use suggestions to improve

### 4. Document Decisions

When an agent helps you make a decision, log it:
1. Use Decision Framer to structure options
2. Make your choice
3. Log in the Decisions tab
4. Set a review date

### 5. Regular Health Checks

Schedule periodic reviews with governance agents:
- Weekly: Strategy Health Monitor
- Monthly: Drift Detector
- Quarterly: Quarterly Review Facilitator

## Troubleshooting

### "No agents found in this category"

This means agents haven't been seeded to the database. Contact your administrator to run the Strategy 120 seed scripts.

### "Connection error"

Check your network connection and try again. If the problem persists, verify the server is running.

### Agent gives generic responses

Ensure your strategic foundation is set up in the Strategy (S2E) page. Agents need context about your vision, mission, and objectives to give relevant answers.
