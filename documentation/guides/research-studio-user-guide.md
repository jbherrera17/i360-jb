# Research Studio - User Guide

**Version:** 1.1.0
**Last Updated:** Wednesday, February 12, 2026

## Overview

Research Studio is a powerful tool for organizing, analyzing, and learning from your documents. Upload sources, ask questions, and generate study materials - all powered by AI.

Think of it as your personal research assistant that:
- Reads and understands your documents
- Answers questions with source citations
- Creates study materials (flashcards, quizzes)
- Generates reports and presentations

---

## Getting Started

### Creating Your First Studio

1. Navigate to **Research Studio** from the Tools menu
2. Click **New Studio**
3. Enter a title (e.g., "Marketing Research")
4. Optionally add a description
5. Click **Create Studio**

You'll be taken to your new studio's workspace.

### Understanding the Interface

The workspace has three panels:

```
┌──────────────┬──────────────────────────────┬───────────────────┐
│   SOURCES    │           CHAT               │      STUDIO       │
│              │                              │                   │
│ Your docs    │ Ask questions here           │ Generate outputs  │
│              │                              │                   │
└──────────────┴──────────────────────────────┴───────────────────┘
```

- **Sources Panel (Left)**: Your uploaded documents
- **Chat Panel (Center)**: Conversation with AI about your sources
- **Studio Panel (Right)**: Tools to generate content

## Adding Sources

### Supported File Types

| Format | Extension | Max Size |
|--------|-----------|----------|
| PDF | .pdf | 50 MB |
| Word | .docx | 50 MB |
| Text | .txt | 50 MB |
| Markdown | .md | 50 MB |
| CSV | .csv | 50 MB |
| Web URL | - | - |

### How to Add Sources

1. Click **Add source** in the Sources panel
2. Choose your method:

**Upload File:**
- Click "Upload File" tab
- Drag and drop or click to browse
- Wait for processing to complete

**Website URL:**
- Click "Website URL" tab
- Paste the full URL
- Click "Add URL"

**Paste Text:**
- Click "Paste Text" tab
- Enter a title
- Paste your content
- Click "Add Text"

### Managing Sources

- **Select/Deselect**: Check the box to include/exclude from AI context
- **Select All**: Use the checkbox at the bottom
- **Delete**: Hover and click the trash icon
- **Download**: Click the source to view download options

## Chatting with Your Sources

### Asking Questions

1. Ensure at least one source is selected (checked)
2. Type your question in the input box
3. Press Enter or click Send

The AI will:
- Search your selected sources
- Find relevant information
- Provide an answer with citations

### Understanding Citations

Responses include numbered citations like `[1]`, `[2]`:

```
According to the research, customer satisfaction increased by 25% [1].
This aligns with the market trends identified in the quarterly report [2].
```

- Click a citation number to highlight the source
- Scroll down to see the full citation with quotes

### Suggested Questions

When you first open a studio with sources, you'll see suggested questions:
- "What are the main themes across these sources?"
- "Summarize the key findings"
- "What questions do these sources answer?"

Click any suggestion to ask that question.

### Follow-up Questions

After receiving an answer, you'll see follow-up suggestions based on the response. Click to continue exploring.

## Tips & Best Practices

### Getting Better Results

1. **Be Specific**: Ask focused questions rather than broad ones
2. **Select Relevant Sources**: Only include sources related to your question
3. **Use Follow-ups**: Build on previous answers for deeper insights
4. **Mix Output Types**: Use reports for analysis, flashcards for learning

### Organizing Your Work

- Create separate studios for different projects
- Use descriptive titles and descriptions
- Delete outdated sources to keep context clean
- Generate outputs before important meetings

### Maximizing AI Quality

- **For Reports**: Add 3-5 substantial sources
- **For Quizzes**: Include diverse content for varied questions
- **For Flashcards**: Sources with clear facts work best
- **For Audio**: Keep source count moderate for coherent narrative

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**"No sources selected"**
- Check that at least one source has a checkmark
- Click "Select all" to include everything

**Source stuck on "Processing"**
- Large files may take longer
- Try refreshing the page
- If persistent, delete and re-upload

**Chat not responding**
- Check your internet connection
- Ensure sources are selected
- Try a simpler question

**Output generation failed**
- Some outputs require minimum content
- Check that sources have text content (not just images)
- Try with fewer sources

### Getting Help

- Click the **info button** (i) in the header for in-app help
- Contact support for persistent issues

## FAQ

**Q: How many sources can I add?**
A: There's no hard limit, but performance is best with 5-15 focused sources.

**Q: Can I share a studio with others?**
A: Not currently. Studios are private to your account.

**Q: Are my sources used to train AI?**
A: No. Your sources are only used to answer your questions in real-time.

**Q: Can I export my outputs?**
A: Audio can be downloaded. Other outputs can be copied or printed from the preview.

**Q: What languages are supported?**
A: Primary support is for English. Other languages may work but with reduced quality.
