Insight 360 is a **full-service enterprise SaaS platform** enabling multi-tenant organizations with subscription tiers, module-based feature access, and agency capabilities. Version 3.26 completes **Phase 44 Enterprise Multi-Tenancy** with full route integration and functional admin platform UI.
