## Executive Summary

Insight 360 is evolving from a multi-LLM orchestration platform into a **values-driven intelligence governance system**. Version 3.4 delivers the **Thought Leadership Module**, a comprehensive system for building professional authority through strategic content creation with AI agents and Notion calendar integration.
