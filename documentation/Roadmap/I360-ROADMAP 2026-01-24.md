## Executive Summary

Insight 360 is evolving from a multi-LLM orchestration platform into a **full-service agency platform**. Version 3.24 delivers the **Agency Model** — enabling consulting agencies to manage multiple clients with white-label branding, client portals, and analytics dashboards.
